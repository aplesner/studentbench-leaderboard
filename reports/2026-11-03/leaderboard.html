Pending

This ranking compares lesson-plan generator systems on GRE Quant for 83 frozen benchmark student contexts: the original 12 systems and the requested new generators. A system includes its model and generation settings. Ranks measure delivered schema-valid plans. Historical baseline plans can include answer-verifier edits; new plans use the released prompt, reconstructed context renderer, and schema/options normalization without that historical answer verifier. The generation pipelines are not historically equivalent, so these rankings describe the recorded generator systems rather than a pure comparison of models. Provider and validation failures are retained in the audit. The AI judge evaluates plan quality; this is not a human grade or a measure of student learning.

Rank 1 is highest estimated quality. Bradley–Terry scores summarize pairwise preferences. Overall rank ranges are 95% student-context bootstrap percentile intervals, conditional on connected, converged fits; they do not establish a unique winner. Missing or abstained judgments are not ties. Criterion-specific fits need not share the overall ordering.

Judge validation and methods

The judge was selected on validation students before examining the held-out test. The held-out comparison below concerns the original 12 generator systems; it is evidence about the judge, not a human grade for new generators.

Pairwise rubric preferences are combined into a Bradley–Terry quality ranking. Ties count as half wins; missing and abstained judgments do not. Student contexts are the resampling unit. Evaluation schedules and model generation settings affect the estimates.

Density analyses are sensitive to opponent schedules, student weights and the fixed Bradley–Terry ridge penalty; stronger relative shrinkage at lower density complicates comparisons. Validation agreement does not establish a certain winner or measure student learning gains.
